Turn on accounts first

The admin console uses the site's accounts to know it's you. They aren't switched on yet.

  1. In Netlify, open the Jachi project, select Identity, then Enable Identity.
  2. Under Registration preferences, choose Invite only for now. Leave email confirmation on.
  3. In Identity, select Invite users and invite support@jachihealth.com. Open the invitation email and choose a password.
  4. Come back here and log in. Then set Registration preferences to Open, so people on your list can make accounts.
