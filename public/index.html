Our approach

Technology that listens. Clinicians who decide.

Jachi uses AI to make care faster and more personal. It never diagnoses, prescribes or provides therapy. Every medical decision is made by a licensed clinician.

How eligibility works

FDA-approved weight-loss medications are generally for adults who meet one of these BMI guidelines:

30+BMI on its own
27+BMI with a weight-related condition

BMI is a screening number, not a measure of your health. A licensed clinician looks at your full health picture before recommending any treatment.

Privacy

Privacy at a glance

The care finder, condition search and weight care check

They run entirely in your browser. What you type or choose, including your height, weight and health details, is never sent to us or stored.

Early access sign-ups

If you join early access, we collect your first name, email, state and the services you're interested in. We use them only to email you about our launch. Sign-ups are stored with our website host.

Help chat and support tickets

Messages you send to our AI assistant pass through our website host, Netlify, to our AI provider, Anthropic, so it can write a reply. We don't save chats. If you send a ticket, we collect your name, email, topic, message and, if you choose, your chat, and use them only to answer you. Tickets are stored with our website host and sent to our support inbox.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, and this site doesn't use advertising cookies or tracking.
