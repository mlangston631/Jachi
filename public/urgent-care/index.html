What to expect from an online urgent care visit.

A licensed clinician leads your care. Here's what happens at your visit, whether you'll need tests, and how follow-up works.

  1. Your visit

    You answer questions about your symptoms online. A licensed clinician reviews them and may message you or start a video visit.

  2. Tests

    Some illnesses, like strep throat, need a quick test. Your clinician will tell you if you need one and where to go.

  3. Follow-up

    If you aren't getting better, or you feel worse, contact your clinician or get seen in person.

Good to know

  • No insurance needed. Any medication is billed separately by your pharmacy.
  • If you need an exam, a test or an X-ray, your clinician will tell you where to go in person.
  • For adults 18 and older. We don't prescribe controlled substances.

Call 911 for an emergency

Jachi isn't for emergencies. Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room for:

  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Trouble breathing
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or trouble speaking
  • Severe bleeding or a serious injury
  • Fainting or a severe allergic reaction

Privacy

Privacy at a glance

The care finder, condition search and weight care check

They run entirely in your browser. What you type or choose, including your height, weight and health details, is never sent to us or stored.

Waitlist sign-ups

If you join the waitlist, we collect your email and state and, if you give them, your first name and the services you're interested in, plus the language of the page you used. If you came from one of our ads, we also note the ad's name. We use them only to email you about our launch. Sign-ups are stored with our website host. If you join through a form in one of our Facebook or Instagram ads, Meta collects what you enter and passes it to us.

Accounts

If you create an account, we keep your email, your password in scrambled form, and the details you save there. Your browser keeps a sign-in token until you log out. You can update or delete your account at any time on the account page.

Help chat and support tickets

Messages you send to our AI assistant pass through our website host, Netlify, to our AI provider, Anthropic, so it can write a reply. We don't save chats. If you send a ticket, we collect your name, email, topic, message and, if you choose, your chat, and use them only to answer you. Tickets are stored with our website host and sent to our support inbox.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, and this site doesn't use advertising cookies, pixels or tracking. We never send your sign-up to Meta or any other ad company.

Your choices

To leave the list or ask about your information, use your account or email support@jachihealth.com, reply to any email from us or use the unsubscribe link in it. Please don't send health details by email.

Your medical information

When you become a patient, your health information is protected under HIPAA and stored in the United States.
