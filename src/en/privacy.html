Privacy policy

Effective October 15, 2026

This policy explains what information Jachi Health ("Jachi," "we," "us") collects through jachihealth.com and the Jachi Health app, how we use it and the choices you have. Jachi isn't open for care yet. When we launch, the care you receive will also be covered by a HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices, which you'll get before your first visit.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Waitlist sign-ups: your email address and state, plus your first name and the services you're interested in if you choose to give them, and the language of the page you used.
  • Accounts: if you create one, your email address, a password (which our hosting provider stores only in scrambled form, so no one can read it), and the details you choose to save: your state, the services you're interested in, the language for our emails and whether you want them. We also keep when you created the account and when you last logged in.
  • Support tickets: your name, email address, topic and message and, if you choose, a copy of your conversation with our AI assistant.
  • Help chat messages: what you type to our AI assistant.
  • Emails you send us.

Information that stays in your browser

The care finder, condition search and weight care check run entirely in your browser. What you enter there, including your height, weight and health answers, is never sent to us or saved.

Information that stays on your phone (the Jachi Health app)

The Jachi Health app for iPhone and Android keeps your health journal on your phone: daily check-ins, symptoms, readings like blood pressure or weight, medications and dose history, reminders, questions for your clinician and notes. It's stored in the app's private storage, and in your phone's own backup if you use one. It isn't sent to us, and we can't see it. The app's care finder and condition search also run on your phone.

Medication and check-in reminders are scheduled by your phone itself; we don't send push notifications. Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint or passcode unlock is handled by your phone, and we never receive it. The visit summary PDF and journal exports are made on your phone and go only where you choose to share them. Deleting the app, or using "Delete all journal data" in its settings, erases the journal from your phone.

The app's waitlist sign-up, account, support tickets and AI guide send the same information as the website's, described above, and are covered by this policy. The AI guide never sends your journal.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and when. It's used to deliver the site, keep it secure and prevent abuse, for example by limiting how many chat messages one visitor can send in a minute. This site doesn't use analytics or advertising trackers, and it loads nothing from other companies' servers. The app doesn't contain analytics, advertising or tracking code either. When it opens, it asks Expo, our app-update service, whether a newer version is ready; that check sends a random ID for your copy of the app, the app version and your phone's platform, and never your name, email or journal. If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your phone's settings, Apple or Google may send us anonymous crash reports.

If you log in to an account, your browser keeps a sign-in token in its local storage, so you stay logged in. Logging out removes it. It's never used for tracking or advertising.

How we use it

  • To email you about Jachi's launch and the services you chose.
  • To run your account, if you have one, including emails that confirm your address or reset your password.
  • To answer your questions and support tickets.
  • To write replies in the help chat.
  • To keep the site secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To understand interest in our services by state and language, so we know where and how to launch.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell it.

Who we share it with

We share information only with companies that help us run the site, and only for that purpose:

  • Netlify hosts the website, stores sign-ups, tickets and accounts, sends account emails, and runs the site's server code.
  • Anthropic provides the AI that writes help chat replies. Your chat messages are sent to Anthropic, through Netlify, to generate each reply.
  • Our email provider hosts our support inbox, where tickets and your emails arrive.
  • Expo builds the Jachi Health app and delivers its updates, as described above.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a merger or sale of our business, in which case this policy would continue to apply. We don't sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

Health information

Please don't send health details through the chat, tickets or email. They aren't meant for medical questions. The health journal in our app stays on your phone, as described above. When Jachi opens and you become a patient, your health information will be protected under HIPAA and handled as described in the Notice of Privacy Practices you'll receive then.

How long we keep it

  • Waitlist sign-ups: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, or once we've opened in your state and you no longer want our emails.
  • Accounts: until you delete your account, which you can do at any time on the account page. Deleting it also removes the waitlist sign-ups and support tickets sent with that email.
  • Support tickets: as long as we need to answer you and keep a record of our support, then we delete them.
  • Chat messages: we don't save chats. If you include your chat in a ticket, it's kept with the ticket.
  • Your app journal: on your phone, until you delete it or the app. We never have a copy.
  • Technical logs: kept by our hosting provider for a limited time under its own policies.

Your choices and rights

If you have an account, you can update your details, turn our emails off or delete your account and the information tied to it at any time at jachihealth.com/account. You can also unsubscribe from our emails with the link in any email or by emailing us. You can also ask us to tell you what personal information we have about you, or to correct or delete it, by emailing {{EMAIL}}. We'll respond within 45 days, and we won't treat you differently for asking. If we can't do what you ask, we'll explain why, and you can appeal by replying to our answer.

Depending on where you live, state laws such as the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act may give you these rights. We honor these requests no matter where you live. Because we don't track visitors across websites or sell personal information, browser signals like Global Privacy Control have nothing to switch off here, but we respect them.

Children

Jachi is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Every page is encrypted with HTTPS, only the people who need sign-ups, tickets and accounts to do their work can see them, and we use providers that protect data with industry-standard safeguards. Please choose an account password you don't use anywhere else. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't send health or financial details through the site or by email.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we'll let people on our email list know.
