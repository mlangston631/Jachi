Privacy policy

Effective November 15, 2026

This policy explains what information Jachi Health ("Jachi," "we," "us") collects through jachihealth.com and the Jachi Health app, how we use it and the choices you have. Jachi coordinates care but doesn't provide it: the providers you see keep your medical records under their own privacy notices.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Waitlist sign-ups: your email address and state, plus your first name and the services you're interested in if you choose to give them, and the language of the page you used. If you came to our site from one of our ads, we also record the ad's name and campaign with your sign-up.
  • Sign-up forms in our Facebook and Instagram ads: if you join the waitlist through one, Meta collects what you enter (your email, first name and state) under its own privacy policy and passes it to us. We use it only as this policy describes.
  • Accounts: if you create one, your email address, a password (which our hosting provider stores only in scrambled form, so no one can read it), and the details you choose to save: your state, the services you're interested in, the language for our emails and whether you want them. We also keep when you created the account and when you last logged in. If you're a member, your account page also shows your plan and invoices. It reads them from Stripe each time you open the page; they aren't saved in your account.
  • Memberships: your name, email address, mobile phone number, preferred language, the plan you choose, who referred you if you tell us, and a record of the terms you agreed to and when. Stripe, our payment processor, collects your card details on its own page: we never see or store your card number. If you turn our member tips emails off, that choice is saved with your membership.
  • Support tickets: your name, email address, topic and message and, if you choose, a copy of your conversation with our AI assistant.
  • Help chat messages: what you type to our AI assistant. Common questions are answered from this website itself, without the AI; if you then ask the assistant something, your conversation so far goes with it so the reply makes sense.
  • Emails you send us.

Information that stays in your browser

Some things are kept only in your own browser, on your own device. They are never sent to us.

  • Your health notebook (the Health section of the account page): the check-ins, readings, visits, prices, questions and notes you enter, for you and for anyone in your household you add. Your browser saves it on that device only. It isn't sent to us or to anyone else, so we can't see it, copy it, restore it or reset its passcode. If you set a passcode, the notebook is encrypted with it before it's saved. A backup file is made by your browser and goes only where you save it. Erasing the notebook, clearing your browser's data for this site, or deleting your account from that device removes it there. On a device other people use, set a passcode.
  • Your member page's lists: the parts of your plan you've ticked off, the notes under "For you" that you've closed, and when you last asked for a call, so the page can show the date we promised to start by.
  • Calendar reminders you add are files your browser makes for your own calendar app. We aren't told about them, and this site doesn't send notifications to your phone.
  • Your help chat stays in the browser tab you're using, so it's still there on the next page, until you close the tab or tap Start over.

Information that stays on your phone (the Jachi Health app)

The Jachi Health app for iPhone and Android keeps your health journal on your phone: daily check-ins, symptoms, readings like blood pressure or weight, medications and dose history, reminders, questions for your clinician and notes. It's stored in the app's private storage, and in your phone's own backup if you use one. It isn't sent to us, and we can't see it. The app's care finder and condition search also run on your phone.

Medication and check-in reminders are scheduled by your phone itself; we don't send push notifications. Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint or passcode unlock is handled by your phone, and we never receive it. The visit summary PDF and journal exports are made on your phone and go only where you choose to share them. Deleting the app, or using "Delete all journal data" in its settings, erases the journal from your phone.

The app's waitlist sign-up, account, support tickets and AI guide send the same information as the website's, described above, and are covered by this policy. The AI guide never sends your journal.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and when. It's used to deliver the site, keep it secure and prevent abuse, for example by limiting how many chat messages one visitor can send in a minute. This site doesn't use analytics or advertising trackers, and it loads nothing from other companies' servers. The app doesn't contain analytics, advertising or tracking code either. When it opens, it asks Expo, our app-update service, whether a newer version is ready; that check sends a random ID for your copy of the app, the app version and your phone's platform, and never your name, email or journal. If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your phone's settings, Apple or Google may send us anonymous crash reports.

If you log in to an account, your browser keeps a sign-in token in its local storage, so you stay logged in, and a number that draws your own leaf on the account page. Logging out removes both. They're never used for tracking or advertising.

If you arrive from one of our ads, the link includes the ad's name and campaign. Your browser keeps them for that visit only, in its session storage, and sends them with the waitlist form if you join. They tell us which ads bring sign-ups. We don't use cookies or pixels for this, and we don't send it to Meta or any other ad company.

How we use it

  • To run your membership: finding, pricing and booking the care you ask for, reminders and follow-up, billing and membership emails, and a few emails a year with tips on using your plan, which you can turn off on your member page.
  • If you joined the waitlist, to email you about Jachi and the services you chose.
  • To run your account, if you have one, including emails that confirm your address or reset your password.
  • To answer your questions and support tickets.
  • To write replies in the help chat.
  • To keep the site secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To understand interest in our services by state and language, so we know where and how to grow.
  • To see which of our ads bring sign-ups, from the ad's name in the link you followed.

We don't sell your information. We don't send it to Meta or any other ad company, and we don't use it to build ad audiences.

Who we share it with

We share information only to provide our service, and only for that purpose:

  • The providers you choose: when your coordinator books or prices care for you, the practice gets what it needs for that, such as your name, contact details and the kind of visit.
  • Netlify hosts the website, stores sign-ups, tickets and accounts, sends account emails, and runs the site's server code.
  • Stripe processes membership payments. It receives your name, email address, phone number, plan and payment details, and emails your invoices and receipts.
  • Our email delivery service sends membership emails, such as your welcome email, payment reminders and cancellation confirmations, and the automatic replies that confirm a waitlist sign-up or a support ticket. It receives your email address and first name, never what you chose or wrote.
  • Anthropic provides the AI that writes help chat replies. Your chat messages are sent to Anthropic, through Netlify, to generate each reply.
  • Our email provider hosts our support inbox, where tickets and your emails arrive.
  • Expo builds the Jachi Health app and delivers its updates, as described above.
  • Meta runs the sign-up forms in our Facebook and Instagram ads. If you use one, Meta collects what you enter and passes it to us. Nothing from our site is sent to Meta.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a merger or sale of our business, in which case this policy would continue to apply. We don't sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

Health information

Please don't send health details through the chat, tickets, membership forms or email; they aren't meant for medical questions. Your coordinator talks with you about the care you need by phone and shares with a provider only what it needs to book you. Your medical records stay with your providers, under their own privacy notices and the laws that apply to them, such as HIPAA. The health journal in our app stays on your phone, and the health notebook on the account page stays in your browser, as described above.

Some of what we collect, like the services you choose, can show that you're looking for health care. Our consumer health data privacy policy explains how we handle it under Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar state laws, and how to see it, delete it or appeal a decision.

How long we keep it

  • Waitlist sign-ups: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, or once we've opened in your state and you no longer want our emails.
  • Accounts: until you delete your account, which you can do at any time on the account page. Deleting it also removes the waitlist sign-ups and support tickets sent with that email.
  • Memberships and payments: while you're a member, then as long as tax and accounting rules require us to keep payment records.
  • Support tickets: as long as we need to answer you and keep a record of our support, then we delete them.
  • Chat messages: we don't save chats. Your browser keeps the conversation in the tab you're using until you close it or tap Start over. If you include your chat in a ticket, it's kept with the ticket.
  • Your health notebook: in your browser, on your device, until you erase it, clear the browser's data for this site or delete your account from that device. We never have a copy.
  • Your app journal: on your phone, until you delete it or the app. We never have a copy.
  • Technical logs: kept by our hosting provider for a limited time under its own policies.

Your choices and rights

If you have an account, you can update your details, turn our emails off or delete your account and the information tied to it at any time at jachihealth.com/account. You can also unsubscribe from our emails with the link in any email or by emailing us. You can also ask us to tell you what personal information we have about you, or to correct or delete it, by emailing {{EMAIL}}. We'll respond within 45 days, and we won't treat you differently for asking. If we can't do what you ask, we'll explain why, and you can appeal by replying to our answer.

Depending on where you live, state laws such as the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act and Washington's My Health My Data Act may give you these rights. We honor these requests no matter where you live. Because we don't track visitors across websites or sell personal information, browser signals like Global Privacy Control have nothing to switch off here, but we respect them.

Children

Jachi is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Every page is encrypted with HTTPS, only the people who need sign-ups, tickets and accounts to do their work can see them, and we use providers that protect data with industry-standard safeguards. Please choose an account password you don't use anywhere else. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't send health or financial details through the site or by email.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we'll let people on our email list know.
