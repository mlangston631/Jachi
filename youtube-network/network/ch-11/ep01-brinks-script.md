# Until Someone Talked, episode 1: Brink's, Boston, 1950

Narration script, first upload. Written 18 Nov 2026 from `verification.md` (which overrides `stories.md` wherever they
differ), the `stories.md` Brink's entry and beat sheet for upload one, `cases.md` entry 1, `market.md`, and
`plan/production-tools.md` section 5. Format follows the sister channel's `network/ch-12/ep01-petters-script.md`.

What this script could and could not rest on: no court opinion in this case was read in full. `verification.md` had
the case names, dates, citations and one-line search snippets of the Massachusetts and First Circuit opinions
(**[CL SNIPPET]**), and the FBI's "Famous Cases" page only as web-search result text (**[SEARCH ONLY]**). Every
quotation below is one of those snippets or search-result lines, word for word as `verification.md` gives it, and each
must be matched against the full page before recording (see the [UNVERIFIED] list). Anything resting on Wikipedia
leads alone is tagged in place, with a fallback line or a cut instruction.

## Title

**Chosen:** The Brink's Robbers Stayed Silent for Six Years. Then One of Them Talked.

Alternatives:
1. Six Years of Silence: The Brink's Robber Who Finally Talked
2. The Biggest Robbery in America at the Time, and the Robber Who Talked

Why: `verification.md` B1, B2 and correction 1. The original "...Then One of Them Was Shot." is dropped because the
shooting (June 1954) did not end the silence; he talked nineteen months later (January 1956). "Solved by a Grudge" is
dropped (B2, B22). Alternative 2 uses "at the time", the only superlative `verification.md` allows; it rests on leads
(B2), so use it only after the FBI page or a 1950 report confirms it (see U2).
