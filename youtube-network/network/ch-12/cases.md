# Crooked Arithmetic: 30 candidate cases

Checked 17 Nov 2026. For each case:

- **Doc** (0-10): depth of primary records. Each was searched on CourtListener (opinions, all statuses); the key
  records found are named. Where a fact came only from a DOJ/FBI release or press found by web search, it says so.
- **Story** (0-10): characters, turns, deception, exposure, payoff.
- **Room** (0-10): YouTube coverage this year, from `ytscout.mjs --period year "<case> documentary"`
  (`scout-cases-a.json`, `scout-cases-b.json`, `scout-extra.json`). 10 = nothing above about 1K views (room for the
  definitive version); low = a strong existing film (red flag). The scout sees only uploads from this year, so
  older classics (CNBC *American Greed*, Netflix, BBC) aren't counted. Each case's notes list the ones I know of.
- **Living-people risk**: who is alive and what is legally settled. High means pending appeals, acquittals,
  fugitives or uncharged people near the story.
- **Ads**: advertiser suitability under YouTube's guidelines (money crime is generally fine; suicide, drugs,
  terrorism finance or violence push it down).
- **Total** = Doc + Story + Room (out of 30). Ranking isn't by fame: famous cases with strong existing films score low
  on Room.

Labels: [conviction], [plea], [acquittal], [court finding], [settlement], [allegation], [reported] (press, not yet
checked against a court record), [verify].

## Case notes

### 1. ADM lysine cartel and Mark Whitacre (1992-1999)
- **Scheme:** ADM and Asian producers fixed lysine prices; the FBI's cooperator inside ADM, Whitacre, was embezzling
  from ADM at the same time.
- **Records (CourtListener):** United States v. Andreas (N.D. Ill. 1998); United States v. Andreas and Wilson (7th Cir.
  2000); Archer Daniels Midland Co. v. Whitacre (C.D. Ill. 1999); Whitacre post-conviction rulings (C.D. Ill. 2001,
  2003). Also DOJ Antitrust Division 1996 release (ADM plea, $100M fine).
- **Outcomes:** ADM pleaded guilty, $100M criminal fine, Oct 1996 [plea]. Michael Andreas and Terrance Wilson
  convicted 1998, imprisoned 1999 [conviction]. Whitacre pleaded guilty to fraud and tax charges and entered prison
  March 1998 on a nine-year sentence [plea; verify the combined term]. The 1992 "extortion" call that brought in the FBI
  is described in a case study as non-existent [reported].
- **YouTube:** best this year 883 views ("The Con Archives"). Known older: the 2009 feature film *The Informant!*.
- **Risk:** Whitacre, Wilson and Andreas may be alive [verify]; all outcomes final. **Ads:** high.

### 2. Tom Petters / Petters Company (c. 1995-2008)
- **Scheme:** forged purchase orders for electronics to be "resold" to warehouse clubs; a $3.5 to 3.7B Ponzi
  structure (figures vary by source) [conviction].
- **Records:** United States v. Petters (8th Cir. 2011, conviction on 20 counts affirmed); United States v. Petters
  (D. Minn. 2013); SEC v. Petters (D. Minn. 2023); In re Petters Co. (Bankr. D. Minn. 2010 to 2019, adversary
  proceedings incl. Kelley v. Hofer); In re GE Capital Corp. / Thomas Petters Investment Litigation (J.P.M.L. 2015).
  FBI Minneapolis releases 3 Dec 2009, 8 Apr 2010, 2 Sept 2010.
- **Outcomes:** convicted Dec 2009, 50 years (Apr 2010) [conviction]. Deanna Coleman turned herself in on 8 Sept
  2008, recorded Petters, pleaded to one conspiracy count, sentenced Sept 2010 [plea]. Raid 24 Sept 2008.
- **YouTube:** best this year 429 views (Five Sigma). Older: CNBC *American Greed* episode [verify].
- **Risk:** Petters alive, in custody; cooperators alive (name them only for their pleas). **Ads:** high.

### 3. Equity Funding Corporation of America (1964-1973)
- **Scheme:** inflated earnings, then about 64,000 fictitious life policies sold on to reinsurers [reported].
- **Records:** In re Equity Funding Corp. of America Securities Litigation (9th Cir. 1979; C.D. Cal. 1975, 1976, 1977);
  In re Equity Funding Corp. of America Litigation (J.P.M.L. 1974, 1975); Equity Funding Corp. v. Loeffler (9th Cir.
  1975); Goldblum v. NBC (9th Cir. 1978: the chairman sued NBC over a televised drama about the case [court record;
  read it]); Dirks v. SEC (D.C. Cir. 1982) and Dirks v. SEC, 463 U.S. 646 (1983). SEC ALJ decision 1978 (sec.gov).
- **Outcomes:** bankruptcy 5 Apr 1973 [reported]; Stanley Goldblum pleaded guilty, eight years [plea]; 22 people
  pleaded guilty or were convicted [reported]. The SEC proceeded against Dirks, the analyst who exposed it; the Supreme
  Court ruled for him in 1983 [court finding].
- **YouTube:** best this year 141 views. **Risk:** low (1973; verify whether principals are alive). **Ads:** high.
