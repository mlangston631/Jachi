## 1. The target

The goal is the fastest growth in YouTube history, and the network should aim at it on purpose. These are the records it is measured against.

**The all-time record. It came from fame.** UR · Cristiano launched on 21 Aug 2024:
- **1M subscribers about 90 minutes after launch.** This comes from news reports ([CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/soccer/news/cristiano-ronaldo-starts-youtube-channel-breaks-record-for-fastest-to-one-million-subscribers), [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/sport/football/cristiano-ronaldo-breaks-world-record-with-new-youtube-channel-1.1724306414865)). Guinness doesn't list it.
- **10M in under a day.** beIN Sports says 11.5 hours; other outlets say "under 12 hours" ([beIN](https://www.beinsports.com/en-us/soccer/articles-video/cristiano-s-channel-breaks-records-on-youtube-2024-10-10)).
- **19,729,827 in 24 hours.** This is the one Guinness certifies, using YouTube's own count ([Guinness](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/655853-most-youtube-subscribers-gained-in-24-hours)).
- **More than 48M after six days** ([Guinness news](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/news/2024/8/cristiano-ronaldo-extends-social-media-domination-as-youtube-channel-smashes-record)), and **50M on 28 Aug 2024, day 7** ([ETV Bharat](https://etvbharat.com/en/!sports/cristiano-ronaldo-becomes-fastest-to-reach-50-million-subscribers-on-youtube-after-achieving-it-in-just-six-days-enn24082805813)).
- **About 60M by 12–13 Sep 2024, days 22–23** ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/amp/sports/2024/9/13/ronaldo-becomes-first-individual-with-one-billion-social-media-followers)).

**The faceless record on the books. It came from rewards that are now banned.** Hamster Kombat's channel was created on 24 May 2024.
- It reached **10M in 6 days, 13 hours and 15 minutes.** That is the team's own claim; it applied to Guinness and I found no approval ([BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/hamster-kombat-guinness-world-record)).
- It had **30.2M by 28 Jun 2024, day 35** ([Invezz](https://invezz.com/news/2024/06/28/hamster-kombat-youtube-channel-is-breaking-records)).
- Players earned in-game coins for subscribing ([Sherwood News](https://sherwood.news/crypto/hamster-kombat-crypto-game-youtube-social-media-growth-hack/)). YouTube's spam policy now prohibits "offering rewards in exchange for likes, views, or subscribers" ([Spam policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973?hl=en), read 16 Nov 2026).
- Its growth stopped. The channel page showed 32.8M on 16 Nov 2026 ([channel](https://www.youtube.com/@HamsterKombat_Official/about)), below the more than 34.4M it had at the end of July 2024 ([Invezz](https://invezz.com/news/2024/07/30/top-hamster-kombat-statistics-ahead-of-the-hmstr-airdrop/)).

**The clean faceless marks: no fame and no rewards.**
- **Skibidi Toilet: nearly 12M new subscribers in about six weeks** in mid-2023 ([Cartoon Brew](https://www.cartoonbrew.com/animators/dafuqboom-youtube-alexey-gerasimov-skibidi-toilet-230239.html)).
  - It came from an original serialized Shorts series. The first episode went up on 7 Feb 2023 ([Mental Floss](https://www.mentalfloss.com/language/what-does-skibidi-mean); [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/skibidi-toilet) says February 2023).
  - The channel passed 36M by 10 Dec 2023 ([ADN/Washington Post](https://adn.com/nation-world/2023/12/10/how-a-toilet-themed-youtube-series-became-the-biggest-thing-online)).
- **Bebefinn: 1M within 100 days and 10M in 14 months** after launching in April 2022 ([Manila Bulletin, 14 Jul 2022](https://mb.com.ph/2022/7/14/pinkfongs-bebefinn-youtube-channel-reaches-one-million-subscribers); [Licensing International, 20 Jun 2023](https://licensinginternational.org/news/pinkfongs-bebefinn-surpasses-10-million-youtube-subscribers-setting-new-company-record/)). Pinkfong's Baby Shark brand was behind it.
- **Largest yearly gain by an animated channel that I have: Cocomelon, about 36M in 2019.** This comes from lists built on Social Blade data ([Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.in/advertising/news/the-21-youtube-channels-that-gained-the-most-subscribers-in-2019-from-t-series-to-mrbeast/articleshow/72946011.cms)).

**The long line.** KIMPRO reached **100M on 17 Apr 2025, about 2 years 8 months after its first upload** in Aug 2022 ([allkpop](https://www.allkpop.com/article/2025/04/kimpro-becomes-the-first-korean-youtubers-to-hit-100-million-subscribers)). It is the fastest from first upload to 100M that I could document. It isn't an official record, and the channel isn't faceless, but its videos have no dialogue.

**The 2026 bar for "fastest-growing channel in the world".** From 1 Jan to 12 Nov 2026, the top three were MrBeast with about 68M, BRUNO with about 26.2M and Alejo Igoa with about 25M ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/top/fastest-growing-youtube-channels/this-year/), third-party estimates).

**The network's goal, as a number and a clock:**
- **Headline: 10,000,000 subscribers on one faceless channel, faster than any faceless channel has done it cleanly.**
  - Rung 1: beat Bebefinn's 14 months.
  - Rung 2: a breakout at or above Skibidi's ~12M in six weeks.
  - Rung 3: beat 6 days 13 hours, the faceless mark that was set with rewards that are now banned.
- **Calendar year: 25M or more**, which would have been top three in the world in 2026.
- **Long line: 100M in under 2 years 8 months.**
- **All-time ceiling: 19,729,827 in 24 hours.**

## 2. How the records were actually set

**Came from fame, rewards or money. None of this is available here.**
- **Fame.** The same pattern shows up for Ronaldo, for JENNIE (1M in about 7 hours, January 2021, [allkpop](https://www.allkpop.com/article/2021/01/blackpinks-jennie-hits-1m-subscribers-on-youtube-within-a-few-hours)) and for Lamine Yamal (about 870K in his first 24 hours, December 2025, [VnExpress](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/sports/football/lamine-yamal-launches-youtube-channel-gains-over-one-million-subscribers-after-two-days-4997659.html)).
  - Fame without strong uploads levels off. UR · Cristiano gained about 60M in its first three weeks, then about 6M in roughly the 11 months to late December 2025, when it had 77.6M ([Bolavip](https://bolavip.com/mx/futbol-internacional/mientras-cristiano-ronaldo-tiene-776-millones-de-suscriptores-la-cifra-que-alcanzo-lamine-yamal-en-su-canal-de-youtube)).
- **Rewards.** Hamster Kombat paid coins to a player base reported at more than 200M. That method is banned (section 6).
- **A parent brand.**
  - Bebefinn launched with Pinkfong's Baby Shark channel behind it, which has 85.4M subscribers today ([channel](https://www.youtube.com/@pinkfong/about)).
  - Cocomelon was bought by Moonbug in July 2020.
- **Paid ads.**
  - A fan analysis counted 428 Google ads in 2025 run by MrBeast's dubbing company. His daily growth fell from about 220K–230K to about 120K a day when the ads paused in April ([Net Influencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/inside-mrbeast-youtube-ad-campaign-that-helped-him-gain-117m-subscribers-in-2025/)).
  - Ads that promote real videos are allowed. But views from ad campaigns don't count toward the Partner Program thresholds ([YPP help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en)). YouTube also adds some ad views to public view counts ([metrics help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2991785?hl=en)).

**Came from format and packaging. All of this is available.**

1. **No language needed.**
   - KIMPRO makes dialogue-free Shorts ([The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/01/21/how-a-south-korean-youtuber-racked-up-100-million-subscribers-without-saying-a-word)). It was the most-viewed channel in the world in 2025 with about 77.53B views (Playboard, via [Korea Times](https://koreatimes.co.kr/entertainment/others/20260108/korean-youtuber-kimpro-takes-no-1-spot-for-annual-global-views)).
   - Alan Becker's channel says: "There is no dialogue, but a lot of humor and emotion." It has 34.4M subscribers and 10.1B views ([channel](https://www.youtube.com/@alanbecker/about), 16 Nov 2026).

2. **A recurring cast and a serial people binge.**
   - Skibidi was built from numbered episodes that end on cliffhangers.
   - Tiny Eraser Studio is the closest live match to our tiny-characters channel. It joined on 1 Jan 2025 and had 932K subscribers, 1,211,883,152 views and 121 videos on 16 Nov 2026 ([channel](https://www.youtube.com/@TinyEraserStudio/about)). It makes wordless Shorts starring stationery characters.
   - Its serial, "Paper's Journey to Defeat Scissors — Part 1–12", shows how a hit works ([Shorts tab](https://www.youtube.com/@TinyEraserStudio/shorts), 16 Nov 2026):
     - Parts 6–8 drew about 0.9M views each.
     - Part 10 drew 21M.
     - Parts 11–12 then drew 1.7M–2.6M, so the hit lifted the floor.
     - A standalone episode, "Eraser's Autograph", has 90M.
   - Serial character channels turn viewers into subscribers more often. Here are lifetime views per subscriber, my arithmetic from channel pages on 16 Nov 2026 (lower means more subscribers per view):

3. **Shorts volume, but quality carries more.**
   - KIMPRO has 4,626 videos since Aug 2022, about 3 a day. Zack D. Films has 4,234 since Jun 2021, about 2.1 a day.
   - Tiny Eraser Studio posted about 1.2 videos a week and still averages about 10M views per video.
   - In vidIQ's study of 10.2M channels (July 2026), median growth was highest (0.91% a month) for channels posting 12 or more times a month. That is a correlation, not proof of cause ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-upload-frequency-study/)).

4. **A format nobody else had.** Skibidi broke out about three months after its first episode. The channel led Tubefilter's chart for May 2023 with 2.8B views that month (per Cartoon Brew).

5. **Riding a trend gives a burst, not lasting growth.** Chief Brioso went from 100K to 1M in 59 days. The second million then took 384 days ([channel milestones](https://www.youtube.com/@chiefbrioso/about)).

6. **Other languages.**
   - YouTube says creators who upload multi-language audio tracks get over 25% of their watch time, on average, in the video's non-primary language. It expanded the feature to millions of creators on 10 Sep 2025 ([YouTube blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/multi-language-audio/)).
   - MrBeast gained more than 47M subscribers from India in 2025, helped by seven audio tracks ([Google India](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/platforms/2025s-biggest-trends-on-youtube-that-gave-india-a-new-shared-vernacular/)).

**Speed through mass-produced AI is not available either.** Pouty Frenchie reached about 2B views within about 7 months of creation. The large AI-only channels were then removed, emptied of videos, or flattened (section 6).

**Why tiny characters is first.** It is the only one of the ten that matches every available mechanic: no language, Shorts-native, a recurring cast, and a serial that turns viewers into subscribers.
- Tiny Eraser Studio proves the format works for a channel started in 2025.
- It also shows the gap. At 932K after about 98 weeks it is a strong channel, but it is far from record pace.
- A tiny-world premise alone is not enough. Minuscule, a wordless TV series about insects, has 1.85M subscribers on an account created in 2006 ([channel](https://www.youtube.com/@Minuscule/about)).

**Why space and science is second.** It can follow the Zack D. Films pattern of 3D "what if" Shorts: 29.7M subscribers in about 5.4 years ([channel](https://www.youtube.com/@zackdfilms/about)).

- Sleep history and rain sound build those hours fastest. Money, AI and tech, company documentaries and insurance and legal terms bring search traffic that keeps coming.

## 4. The moonshot play

### Channel 1: wordless tiny characters

**Format.**
- Wordless animated Shorts, using sound effects and music instead of words.
- A cast of 3–5 tiny characters. Each one has a single want and a silhouette you can read at phone size.
- One home world everyone recognizes, such as a kitchen counter, a desk drawer or a garden bed. Pick a world that Tiny Eraser Studio (stationery) and Minuscule (insects) don't use.
- Own every character, rig, model, song and sound outright. Skibidi's creator didn't own the song or the models he used ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/news/skibidi-toilet-creator-sends-copyright-strikes-to-gametoons-despite-not-owning-the-song-or-models-in-his-videos)), and the rights later ended up in court ([Cartoon Brew](https://www.cartoonbrew.com/law/skibidi-toilet-ip-battle-invisible-narratives-dafuqboom-263954.html)).

**Series concept.** Run two tracks.
- **Standalone gags.** Each one must be a different story. YouTube's policy explicitly allows "a series following a set of characters across episodes… in which each video has a distinct storyline". It explicitly bars "characters put in the same situation over and over again with the same outcome" ([monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)).
- **A numbered serial quest of 10–20 parts.** Each part ends on a cliffhanger, and the next arc starts the day the last one ends. Skibidi's numbered episodes and Tiny Eraser Studio's Part 10 (21M views) are the evidence for this.

**Shorts strategy.**
- Gags run 20–60 seconds. Serial parts can run longer, since Shorts may be up to 3 minutes from 15 Oct 2024 ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/10/03/youtube-shorts-three-minutes-max-length-redesign-trends-templates/)).
- Use the same serial title plus the part number on every episode, and point every episode back to Part 1 with a pinned comment and a playlist.
- Post a "Best of" long-form compilation every month. Tiny Eraser Studio's 514-second "Best of Rock Paper Scissors Animation" had 8.9M views at about 300 days old (YouTube search, 16 Nov 2026). Compilations build the long-form hours that Shorts can't.
- Set the audience honestly. YouTube lists "animated content that appeals to everyone" as general audience. It also counts "animated characters or cartoon figures" that appeal to children as a sign of made-for-kids content ([made-for-kids help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9528076?hl=en)). Write the humor for adults and children alike.
- The "wordless" label is not the hook. In a YouTube search on 16 Nov 2026, almost every recent upload titled "A Wordless Animated Short Film" had under 1,000 views. The exception was an Oscar-nominated film.

**Upload pace a human-edited team can keep.**
- Launch with a bank of 12–20 finished episodes. Ronaldo opened with 19 videos, and depth matters when a viewer arrives and wants more.
- Post 3 Shorts a week, which is 12 or more a month, vidIQ's top band.
- Keep about two weeks of finished episodes in reserve at all times, so a breakout can be fed every day for six weeks without dropping quality.
- Go to daily only while every episode stays a distinct story.

**When to pour effort in.**
1. **Now until 1 Feb 2027 (77 days).**
   - Today's bar to join the Partner Program through Shorts is 1,000 subscribers plus 10M qualified Shorts views in 90 days.
   - Creators who apply from 1 Feb 2027 need 20M Shorts views in 90 days. Creators already in the program aren't affected ([YouTube blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/)).
   - Aim the launch at clearing today's bar before that date.
2. **The breakout trigger.** Watch for a Short running at 10 times or more the channel's median views within 48 hours. When it happens:
   - publish the next part within 24–48 hours, then one a day from the reserve;
   - pin Part 1;
   - cut a compilation within the week;
   - make the next arc about the character that broke out.
   - The window lasts weeks. Skibidi's ran about six weeks and Chief Brioso's 59 days.
3. **Calendar moments, but only as a supplement.** Tiny Eraser Studio's Halloween Shorts drew 149K–281K views, while its football Short drew 4.7M. The serial is the main bet. Use only your own characters, never a league's or a tournament's marks.

### Channel 2: space and science

**Format.**
- 3D "what if", "how big" and "how it works" Shorts under one numbered series name.
- Optionally one recurring non-human character, such as a tiny probe.
- One long-form deep dive every two weeks, because long-form turns viewers into subscribers (Kurzgesagt's 156 views per subscriber).

**Language.** Add multi-language audio tracks to the best Shorts and to all long-form videos (over 25% of watch time, on average, in non-primary languages, per YouTube).

**Accuracy.** Check every fact. Disclose realistic AI scenes of real places or events. YouTube's own example of what needs a label is "a tornado moving toward a real town" ([YouTube blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/disclosing-ai-generated-content)).

**Pace.** Post 3–5 Shorts a week and 2 long-form videos a month. Zack D. Films' 2.1 a day shouldn't be copied with templates, because that runs into the inauthentic-content rule (section 6).

**When to pour effort in.** Sky events and launches are scheduled months ahead, so prepare for them. For example, the total solar eclipse of 2 Aug 2027 crosses southern Spain and has over six minutes of totality near Luxor ([IGN Spain](https://astronomia.ign.es/en/web/guest/eclipses-de-sol-y-luna/eclipse-total-sol-de-2-de-agosto-2027)).

## 6. The rules that can't be broken on the way

1. **No rewards for engagement, and no sub-for-sub.**
   - YouTube bans "content that solely exists to incentivize viewers for engagement", and "channels that don't follow this policy may be terminated". A hired promoter's methods count against the channel ([fake engagement policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3399767?hl=en)).
   - Hamster Kombat's method is banned now. Its subscribers stopped growing in 2024.
2. **No mass-produced or templated episodes.**
   - Since 15 Jul 2025, monetized content must not be "mass-produced, generic, repetitive, or manipulative" ([monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)).
   - The policy also names "repetitive scenarios of animals in exaggerated distress or peril" and "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates".
   - It does allow "using AI to visualize a unique character and narrative you invented".
   - In late January 2026, 16 AI channels with about 35M subscribers combined were deleted or emptied of videos ([Net Influencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/youtube-removes-major-ai-slop-channels-with-combined-9-8m-in-estimated-annual-revenue/)). Super Cat League lost all its videos.
3. **No misleading titles, and no one else's footage or characters.** Screen Culture and KH Studio were terminated in December 2025 for "spam and misleading metadata" ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/industry-news/tech/youtube-terminates-screen-culture-kh-studio-over-ai-fake-movie-trailers/)).
4. **Never present invented events as real, and no fake news about real people.**
   - True Crime Case Files was terminated in January 2025 ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/02/14/youtube-true-crime-case-files-ai-crime-channel-terminated/)).
   - YouTube removed 16 of the AI channels that posted about the "Diddy" trial ([Indicator](https://indicator.media/p/youtube-diddy-trial-ai-slop-bieber-50cent-jamie-foxx-wendy-williams)).
   - Label all fiction clearly.
5. **Children.**
   - Set made-for-kids honestly. It is a legal duty under COPPA, and Google and YouTube paid $170M in 2019 over it ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2019/09/google-youtube-will-pay-record-170-million-alleged-violations-childrens-privacy-law)).
   - Never put disturbing themes into animation that appeals to children. In November 2017 YouTube terminated more than 270 accounts and removed more than 150,000 videos ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2017/11/28/youtube-sexualised-child-imagery-response/)), and in 2025 it terminated AI channels that copied the pattern ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/05/02/ai-elsagate-videos-minion-go-cat-child-safety-content/)).
6. **Disclose realistic AI.** Since May 2026 YouTube adds the label itself when it detects undisclosed photorealistic AI, and some disclosures can't be removed ([YouTube blog, 27 May 2026](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/improving-ai-labels-for-viewers-and-creators/)).
7. **Income channels.**
   - Sleep history must not be "readings of other materials you did not originally create".
   - Rain sound must not be content that "feels interchangeable from video to video" (both quotes from the monetization policies).
8. **Paid promotion: real videos only.** Views from ad campaigns don't count toward the Partner Program thresholds.
9. **Money floor from 1 Feb 2027.** Shorts ad revenue requires 10M qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days. Below that, a channel stays in the program but earns only from long-form until it crosses 10M again.

## 7. One line on odds
